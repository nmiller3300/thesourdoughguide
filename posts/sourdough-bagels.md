# Sourdough Bagels

A real bagel is a different thing from what most grocery stores sell. It should be dense and chewy — almost resistant — with a glossy, slightly blistered crust and a crumb that holds up to cream cheese without going soggy. The secret is boiling before baking, and sourdough starter makes them even better than the commercial-yeast version.

These take two days but most of that is hands-off. The results are extraordinary.

## Ingredients

*(Makes 8 bagels)*

**For the dough:**
- **500g** bread flour (high protein is essential — bagels need strong gluten)
- **270g** water, room temperature
- **120g** active sourdough starter
- **10g** fine sea salt
- **15g** honey or barley malt syrup (adds flavor and browning)
- **10g** neutral oil

**For the boiling water:**
- Large pot of water
- **2 tbsp** baking soda (creates alkaline environment for the glossy crust)
- **1 tbsp** honey or barley malt syrup

**Toppings (choose):**
- Everything bagel seasoning
- Sesame seeds (toasted)
- Poppy seeds
- Coarse salt
- Dried onion or garlic flakes
- Plain (no topping)

## Method

### Day 1 — Mix, Ferment, Shape

**Mix the dough:**
1. Combine all dough ingredients and mix until a cohesive dough forms. Bagel dough is intentionally stiff — it should feel like firm Play-Doh, not soft bread dough. This is correct.

2. Knead for **8–10 minutes** by hand (or 5 minutes in a stand mixer with a dough hook) until smooth, tight, and elastic.

3. Shape into a ball, place in a lightly oiled bowl, cover, and ferment at room temperature **4–8 hours** until about 50% increased.

**Shape the bagels:**
The classic method:
1. Divide dough into **8 equal pieces** (about 115g each).
2. Shape each piece into a smooth, tight ball.
3. Poke your thumb through the center of each ball.
4. Using both hands, rotate the dough around your fingers, gradually stretching the hole until it's about 2 inches in diameter. The hole will shrink during proofing and baking, so make it larger than you think.

**Important:** All 8 bagels should look uniform — same size hole, same tension. Misshapen bagels at this stage will be misshapen after baking.

**Cold retard:**
Place shaped bagels on a parchment-lined baking sheet, lightly dusted with cornmeal or semolina (prevents sticking). Cover with plastic wrap and **refrigerate overnight** (12–24 hours). The cold proof develops flavor and makes the dough more cooperative for boiling.

---

### Day 2 — Boil and Bake

**Float test before boiling:**
Drop one bagel in a bowl of water. If it floats immediately, they're ready. If it sinks and doesn't float within 10 seconds, they need more time at room temperature (30 minutes to 1 hour).

**Preheat oven to 450°F (230°C)** with a rack in the upper third.

**Boil:**
1. Bring a large pot of water to a rolling boil.
2. Add baking soda and honey. The water will foam briefly — this is fine.
3. Boil bagels **30 seconds per side** (1 minute total) for a chewier texture, or **45 seconds per side** for a slightly softer bite.
4. Don't overcrowd the pot — boil 2–3 at a time.
5. Remove with a slotted spoon and place back on the cornmeal-dusted parchment.

**Top immediately:**
While still wet from boiling, dip the top of each bagel in your chosen toppings or sprinkle them on. The wet surface acts as glue. Press gently to adhere.

**Bake:**
Bake at **450°F for 18–22 minutes** until deep golden brown all over. Rotate the pan halfway through for even coloring.

The bagels should look darker than you expect — pale bagels are underbaked.

**Cool 15 minutes** before cutting. Hot bagels are gummy inside.

## The Perfect Everything Bagel Seasoning

Make your own — it's better:
- 2 tbsp poppy seeds
- 2 tbsp sesame seeds (toasted)
- 1 tbsp dried onion flakes
- 1 tbsp dried garlic flakes
- 1 tbsp coarse sea salt

Mix and store in a jar. Use generously.

## Serving

The classic: toasted, cream cheese, smoked salmon, capers, thin red onion, fresh dill.

But also:
- Egg and cheese sandwich (breakfast bagel)
- Tuna salad with everything bagel seasoning
- Avocado and everything seasoning
- Lox and whitefish salad
- Just butter. Good butter. Nothing else.

## Storage

Bagels are best the day they're made. By day 2, toast them.

**Freezing:** Slice before freezing. Freeze in a zip-lock bag. Toast from frozen directly — 3–4 minutes in a toaster or 8 minutes at 375°F in the oven.

Frozen bagels toasted are genuinely better than room-temperature day-old bagels. Freeze them while fresh.
