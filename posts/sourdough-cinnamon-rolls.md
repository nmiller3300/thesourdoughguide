# Sourdough Cinnamon Rolls

These are not your average cinnamon rolls. The sourdough starter adds a subtle tang that balances the sweetness perfectly, and the long overnight fermentation develops a depth of flavor that instant yeast simply can't match. They're also notably fluffier — the long, slow rise builds a more tender, pillowy crumb.

Yes, they take overnight. Yes, they are absolutely worth it.

## Ingredients

**For the dough:**
- **500g** all-purpose flour
- **200g** warm whole milk (about 110°F)
- **150g** active sourdough starter
- **80g** unsalted butter, softened
- **60g** granulated sugar
- **2** large eggs, room temperature
- **8g** fine sea salt
- **1 tsp** vanilla extract

**For the filling:**
- **80g** unsalted butter, softened (not melted)
- **150g** brown sugar, packed
- **2.5 tbsp** ground cinnamon
- **Pinch** of fine salt

**For the cream cheese frosting:**
- **225g** cream cheese, softened
- **120g** powdered sugar, sifted
- **3 tbsp** heavy cream or milk
- **1 tsp** vanilla extract
- **Pinch** of salt

**Yield:** 12 large rolls

---

## Method

### Day 1 — Make the Dough

**In a large bowl or stand mixer:**

1. Combine warm milk, starter, eggs, sugar, and vanilla. Mix well.

2. Add flour and salt. Mix until a shaggy dough forms.

3. Add softened butter, a few tablespoons at a time, mixing after each addition. This takes patience — the butter will seem like it won't incorporate, then suddenly it will. Continue mixing (or kneading by hand) for **8–10 minutes** until the dough is smooth, soft, and slightly tacky but not sticky.

4. Place in a lightly oiled bowl. Cover and let rise at room temperature until about **50% increased in volume** — this can take **4–8 hours** depending on your starter's activity and room temperature.

5. Once risen, cover tightly and **refrigerate overnight** (8–16 hours). Cold dough is much easier to roll and fill.

---

### Day 2 — Fill, Cut, and Bake

**Remove dough from fridge and let rest 20 minutes** while you make the filling.

**Make filling:** Beat softened butter, brown sugar, cinnamon, and salt together until smooth. It should be a thick, spreadable paste.

**Roll the dough:**
1. Lightly flour your work surface.
2. Roll cold dough into a **14×18 inch rectangle** (about ¼ inch thick). The cold temperature makes this much easier.
3. Spread filling evenly over the surface, leaving a ½-inch border on one long edge.

**Cut rolls:**
1. Starting from the filled long edge, roll the dough tightly toward the bare edge.
2. Pinch the seam closed.
3. Cut into **12 equal pieces** (about 1.5 inches each) using a sharp knife or unflavored dental floss.

**Second rise:**
1. Arrange rolls cut-side up in a buttered 9×13 pan, leaving space between them.
2. Cover loosely and let rise at room temperature until puffy and touching each other — **2–4 hours**. They should look noticeably bigger and jiggly.

**Bake:**
Preheat oven to **375°F (190°C)**.

Bake rolls **22–28 minutes** until golden on top and a thermometer inserted in the center reads 185–190°F. Don't underbake — undercooked cinnamon rolls are doughy and raw-tasting in the center.

**Make frosting:** Beat cream cheese until smooth. Add powdered sugar, cream, vanilla, and salt. Beat until light and fluffy.

**Frost:** Spread frosting generously over rolls while still warm (not hot). The frosting will melt slightly into the swirls — this is exactly what you want.

## Tips

**Starter ripeness matters.** Use starter that's active but not at its absolute peak — a slightly past-peak starter produces a more complex, tangy flavor that works beautifully in enriched doughs.

**Don't melt the butter for the filling.** Softened butter stays in place. Melted butter runs out everywhere and makes a mess.

**Dental floss for cutting.** Slide unflavored floss under the roll, cross the ends over the top, and pull. Cleaner cuts than a knife.

**Make-ahead option:** After cutting and placing in the pan, cover tightly and refrigerate instead of room-temperature rising. The next morning, pull from the fridge 1–2 hours before baking to warm up, then bake as directed.

**Reheating:** 30 seconds in the microwave with a damp paper towel. Or wrap in foil and heat at 325°F for 10 minutes. Add extra frosting after reheating.

---

## Variations

**Apple cinnamon:** Spread thin-sliced, cinnamon-sugared apples over the filling before rolling.

**Cardamom rolls (Swedish-style):** Replace half the cinnamon with ground cardamom. Reduce sugar slightly. Don't frost — brush with simple syrup and top with pearl sugar.

**Chocolate:** Replace filling with Nutella or a mixture of brown sugar, cocoa powder, and butter.
