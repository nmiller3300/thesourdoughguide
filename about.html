About The Sourdough Guide

Built for bakers, by people who love bread.

Why We Built This

Great sourdough information exists on the internet — but it's scattered. Buried in Reddit threads, conflicting with forum posts from 2009, impossible to follow for beginners, and overwhelming even for experienced bakers.

The Sourdough Guide exists to be one clear, reliable, well-organized place to find everything about sourdough baking. Whether you're feeding your first starter or perfecting your scoring technique, everything you need is here.

"Good bread takes time. Learning to make good bread shouldn't."

What We Cover

We cover the full spectrum of sourdough — from complete beginner guides that assume zero baking knowledge, to advanced techniques for experienced bakers looking to take their craft to the next level.

  • Step-by-step beginner guides
  • Troubleshooting for every common failure
  • Recipes from classic country loaves to specialty breads
  • Honest equipment reviews and recommendations
  • The science behind fermentation, hydration, and gluten
