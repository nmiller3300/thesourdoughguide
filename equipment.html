🧺 Bannetons & Proofing

Nice to Have
✓
Nice to Have $15–25
Round Rattan Banneton (9 inch)
For standard 800g–1kg round loaves. Rattan wicks moisture and leaves those beautiful spiral rings on the crust. Get a linen liner — it prevents sticking better than bare rattan.
~$18
⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
Our Verdict
An actual banneton makes shaping much easier and gives your loaves a professional look. You can substitute a floured bowl with a kitchen towel, but a banneton is better.
✅ Pros
  • Wicks moisture
  • Beautiful ring pattern
  • Inexpensive
  • Lasts years
⚠️ Cons
  • Needs proper flouring
  • Requires drying between uses

✓
Nice to Have $8–15
Bench Scraper (Metal)
The most underrated tool in sourdough baking. Essential for handling sticky dough, dividing, and shaping. Get a metal one with a comfortable grip.
~$10
⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
Our Verdict
If you bake high-hydration dough and don't own a bench scraper, get one immediately. It's the difference between shaping feeling impossible and feeling manageable.
✅ Pros
  • Very cheap
  • Essential for shaping
  • Also great for cleaning
⚠️ Cons
  • Nothing significant

Must Have $10–30
Bread Lame (Scoring Tool)
A sharp blade on a handle for scoring. A basic razor blade works, but a proper lame gives you more control for decorative scoring and consistent cuts.
~$12–25
⭐⭐⭐⭐
Our Verdict
Start with a simple straight lame. Only upgrade to a curved lame if you want to do decorative scoring — the basic version works perfectly for every functional purpose.
✅ Pros
  • Precise control
  • Replaceable blades
  • Lasts forever
⚠️ Cons
  • Blades are sharp — handle carefully
