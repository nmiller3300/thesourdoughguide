Tools of the Trade

Honest recommendations at every price point — only what actually makes a difference to your bread.

Must Have

Dutch Oven

A Dutch oven traps steam during baking, giving your loaf the environment it needs to spring and develop a proper crust. Without one, home ovens simply cannot replicate what a professional steam-injected deck oven does. This is the single most impactful piece of equipment you can buy.

Best Overall
Lodge Enameled Cast Iron (6 qt)

The recommendation we give to almost everyone. Heavy, retains heat perfectly, enamel makes cleanup easy. Lasts a lifetime.

Must Have

Kitchen Scale

Baking by weight is non-negotiable in sourdough. Volume measurements are too imprecise — a cup of flour can vary by 30% depending on how it's scooped. A kitchen scale eliminates all of that variability and makes every recipe reproducible.

Best Value

Pull-out display stays visible under large bowls. Accurate to 1 gram. Simple, reliable, and built to last.

Nice to Have

Banneton

A banneton holds your shaped dough during its final proof, giving it structure and that beautiful spiral pattern. You can substitute a floured bowl lined with a kitchen towel to start — but a banneton makes the whole process significantly easier.

Top Pick
Round Rattan Banneton (9 inch)

For a standard 900g–1kg loaf. Rattan wicks moisture and leaves beautiful rings. Get the linen liner too.
