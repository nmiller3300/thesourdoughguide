Must Have

Dutch Oven

The single most important piece of equipment for home sourdough. A Dutch oven traps steam during baking, which keeps the crust soft long enough for maximum oven spring — then you remove the lid to get that deep brown, crackly crust.

Without a Dutch oven, home ovens simply can't produce the steam needed for proper sourdough crust. Everything else on this list is secondary to getting a good Dutch oven.

Must Have

Kitchen Scale

Baking by weight is non-negotiable in sourdough. Volume measurements (cups, tablespoons) are too imprecise — 1 cup of flour can vary by 30% depending on how it's scooped. A cheap digital scale solves this entirely.

Nice to Have

Banneton (Proofing Basket)

A banneton holds your shaped dough during its final proof, giving it structure and that beautiful spiral pattern. You can use a floured bowl lined with a towel to start — but a banneton makes shaping much easier.

Top Pick

For a standard 900g–1kg loaf. Rattan wicker wicks away moisture and leaves beautiful rings. Get the liner cloth too.

Optional

Bread Lame (Scoring Tool)

A lame is a razor blade on a handle used to score your dough before baking. You can use a sharp serrated knife or razor blade to start — but a lame gives you more control for detailed scoring patterns.
