Troubleshooting

Something Went Wrong?

Find your problem below and fix it fast — we've seen every sourdough failure and know exactly what causes them.

My sourdough is too dense / gummy inside

Most likely cause: Under-fermentation. Your dough didn't have enough time to develop gas bubbles before baking.

  • Check that your starter is truly active — it should double within 4–6 hours of feeding
  • Extend your bulk fermentation time, especially in cool kitchens
  • Look for 50–75% volume increase and a domed top before shaping
  • Make sure you bake from cold (straight from fridge) for best results
My loaf came out flat with no oven spring

Most likely cause: Over-proofing or a weak starter.

  • Test your starter with the float test before using it
  • If over-proofed, the dough loses its structure — it will spread sideways instead of up
  • Make sure your Dutch oven is screaming hot before the dough goes in
  • Score deep enough — at least ¼ inch — to give steam a release point
My starter isn't bubbling or rising

Most likely cause: It needs more time, warmer temps, or a different flour.

  • Keep your starter somewhere warm — 75–80°F is ideal
  • Try whole wheat or rye flour, which has more wild yeast
  • Feed more consistently — same time every day
  • Make sure you're not using chlorinated tap water (use filtered)
My bread is way too sour

Most likely cause: Over-fermentation, or fermenting in too cool a temperature for too long.

  • Shorten your bulk fermentation time
  • Use your starter earlier (just after peak, not past it)
  • Cold proof for less time
  • Use a higher ratio of starter for faster, less acidic fermentation
My dough is way too sticky to handle

Most likely cause: High hydration or under-developed gluten.

  • Wet hands instead of floured hands — flour makes stickiness worse
  • Make sure you complete all stretch-and-fold sets during bulk fermentation
  • Try reducing hydration by 5–10% on your next bake
  • Bench rest longer before final shaping
My crust is too hard / too thick

Most likely cause: Too long uncovered in the oven, or cooling in a closed space.

  • Reduce uncovered baking time by 5 minutes
  • Cool on a wire rack with airflow — not in a bag or covered
  • Lower oven temp slightly for the uncovered portion
My crust isn't browning / looks pale

Most likely cause: Oven too cool or not enough time uncovered.

  • Make sure your oven is fully preheated to 500°F (or as high as it goes)
  • Preheat your Dutch oven inside the oven for at least 45 minutes
  • Extend the uncovered portion of baking by 5–10 minutes
My starter has mold / smells really bad

Most likely cause: Contamination or too long without feeding.

  • Pink, orange, or fuzzy growth = throw it out and start over
  • Liquid on top (hooch) is normal — stir it in or pour off, then feed
  • A sharp, acidic smell is fine. Rotten or putrid smell = start over
  • Always use a clean jar and clean tools
