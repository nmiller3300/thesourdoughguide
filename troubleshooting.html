Troubleshooting

Something Went Wrong?

Find your problem below and fix it fast — every common sourdough failure, diagnosed and solved.

My sourdough is too dense or gummy inside+

Most likely cause: Under-fermentation — the dough didn't develop enough gas before baking.

  • Check your starter is truly active — it must double within 4–6 hours of feeding
  • Extend bulk fermentation time, especially in cool kitchens
  • Look for 50–75% volume increase before shaping
  • Always bake straight from the fridge (cold proof)
My loaf came out flat with no oven spring+

Most likely cause: Over-proofing, weak starter, or oven/Dutch oven not hot enough.

  • Test starter with float test before using
  • Make sure Dutch oven preheats for 45+ minutes at 500°F
  • Score at least ½ inch deep at a 30–45° angle
  • If over-proofed, shorten cold proof next time
My starter isn't bubbling or rising+

Most likely cause: Temperature too cool, chlorinated water, or needs more time.

  • Keep starter at 75–80°F — cool kitchens slow yeast dramatically
  • Use filtered water or water left out overnight
  • Try whole wheat or rye flour for a few feedings
  • Feed daily at the same time and be patient
My bread tastes way too sour+

Most likely cause: Over-fermentation or too long a cold proof.

  • Shorten bulk fermentation time
  • Use starter just at peak — not past it
  • Reduce cold proof to 8 hours instead of 16
  • Use a higher ratio of starter for faster, less acidic fermentation
My dough is too sticky to handle+

Most likely cause: High hydration or under-developed gluten.

  • Use wet hands instead of floured hands
  • Complete all stretch-and-fold sets during bulk fermentation
  • Try reducing hydration by 5–10% on your next bake
  • Extend bench rest before final shaping
My crust is too hard or too thick+

Most likely cause: Too long uncovered in the oven, or improper cooling.

  • Reduce uncovered baking time by 5 minutes
  • Cool on a wire rack with good airflow — never in a bag or sealed container
  • Lower the uncovered oven temp by 25°F
My starter has mold or smells terrible+

Key distinction: Pink, orange, or fuzzy growth = throw it out. Liquid on top (hooch) = normal, stir it in.

  • A sharp, tangy smell is fine. Rotten or putrid = start over
  • Always use a clean jar and clean utensils
  • Feed more frequently to prevent neglect
